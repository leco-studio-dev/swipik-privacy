SwiPik Privacy Policy

Local processing

SwiPik processes permitted photos and videos locally. We do not operate a server for uploading media.

Meta App Events

Only if you separately opt in in SwiPik, Meta App Events sends app-install and app-open information, device and advertising identifiers, and network information to Meta to measure and optimize Facebook and Instagram ads. SwiPik does not send media content, filenames, album names, swipe directions, cleanup details, or custom Meta events. See Meta’s Privacy Policy. This measurement is off by default, has its own Allow / Not now choice, and can be revoked with the Facebook and Instagram ad measurement switch in Settings. It is separate from Google UMP consent and is not personalized-ads consent.

Other services and choices

Google services are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. You can change local permissions, consent, advertising ID, and app data in Android settings.
