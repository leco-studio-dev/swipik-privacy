SwiPik Privacy Policy

Local processing

SwiPik processes permitted photos and videos locally on your device. We do not operate a developer media or account server.

Meta App Events

Only if you separately opt in in SwiPik, Meta App Events sends app-install and app-open information, device and advertising identifiers, and network information to Meta to measure and optimize Facebook and Instagram ads. SwiPik does not send media content, filenames, album names, swipe directions, cleanup details, or custom Meta events. See Meta’s Privacy Policy. This measurement is off by default, has its own Allow / Not now choice, and can be disabled at any time with the Facebook and Instagram ad measurement switch in Settings. It is separate from Google UMP consent and is not personalized-ads consent.

Other services and choices

Google services are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. You can change local permissions, consent, advertising ID, and app data in Android settings.

Data deletion and privacy assistance

Your media and app preferences remain on your device. You can delete media with Android controls, clear SwiPik app data, or uninstall the app. For privacy questions or help with deletion, contact leco.studio.dev@gmail.com.
