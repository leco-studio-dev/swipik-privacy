Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

SwiPik ("we", "our", or "the app") is a gallery cleaner app that helps you organize photos and videos on your Android device. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

SwiPik does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. All data stays on your device.

  • Photos and Videos: SwiPik accesses your device's media library solely to display, organize, and delete files as directed by you. No media is uploaded or shared.
  • App Preferences: Gesture settings and session data are stored locally using Android DataStore. This data never leaves your device.

Advertising

SwiPik uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this data.

Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: To display your photos and videos for review.
  • WRITE_MEDIA_IMAGES / WRITE_MEDIA_VIDEO: To move or delete files as you request.
  • INTERNET: Required for AdMob advertisements only.

Data Retention

SwiPik does not retain any user data. All operations (delete, favorite, skip) are performed locally on your device. Deleted files are moved to your device's trash and are subject to your system's retention policy.

Children's Privacy

SwiPik is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
